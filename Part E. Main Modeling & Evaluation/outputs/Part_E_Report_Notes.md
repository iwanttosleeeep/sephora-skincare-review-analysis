# Part E report notes: main model and evaluation

All numbers below are generated by `03_main_model_E.ipynb`; rerun the notebook to refresh them.

## Data and model

- Reviews: 1,093,847; products (clusters): 2,351; five-star rate: 63.9%.
- Main model: logistic regression of `five_star` on price segment × skin type, category and nine highlight indicators. References: Budget price, Normal skin, Moisturizers.
- Standard errors are clustered by product. Reduced model: identical without the interaction.
- Full coefficients, clustered standard errors, odds ratios, 95% CIs and p-values: `odds_ratios_main_model.csv`.

Category and highlight odds ratios hold the other variables constant. Because of the interaction, the price odds ratios describe Normal-skin reviewers and the skin-type odds ratios describe Budget products; use the predicted probabilities below to discuss price and skin type.

These are associations in observational data, not causal effects.

## Skin type × price segment interaction

- Joint Wald test of the 12 interaction terms: χ²(12) = 11.33, p = 0.5005.
- There is insufficient evidence that the association between price segment and five-star ratings differs by skin type.
- 1 of 12 individual interaction terms has p < 0.05, about what chance alone would produce (0.6 expected). They are not interpreted individually.

## Predictive performance on held-out products

Split: 80/20 by product (seed 5106); 884,579 training and 209,268 test reviews; no product appears in both sets.

| Model | Threshold | ROC-AUC | Balanced accuracy | Macro F1 | Accuracy | Share predicted five-star |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline (always five-star) | n/a | 0.500 | 0.500 | 0.386 | 0.630 | 100.0% |
| Reduced model | 0.5 (default) | 0.524 | 0.500 | 0.386 | 0.630 | 100.0% |
| Reduced model | 0.641 (training five-star rate) | 0.524 | 0.508 | 0.499 | 0.507 | 49.5% |
| Main model | 0.5 (default) | 0.524 | 0.500 | 0.386 | 0.630 | 100.0% |
| Main model | 0.641 (training five-star rate) | 0.524 | 0.508 | 0.500 | 0.509 | 50.4% |

- ROC-AUC is 0.524 for the main model and 0.524 for the reduced model, against 0.500 for the baseline: the model ranks five-star reviews only slightly better than chance.
- At the 0.5 threshold every predicted probability is above 0.5, so the model labels all test reviews five-star and its threshold-based metrics are identical to the baseline's.
- At the training five-star rate (0.641), macro F1 rises to 0.500 (baseline 0.386) but accuracy falls to 0.509, because many five-star reviews fall just below the cut-off.

## How to phrase the conclusion

- Product category and some product claims are associated with the likelihood of a five-star review, but these associations are modest.
- The same variables have weak out-of-sample predictive power: product attributes explain little of whether an individual reviewer gives five stars.
- There is no evidence that the price–rating association differs by skin type.
- Avoid causal wording such as "Clean at Sephora products cause lower ratings"; use "are associated with".
